About this app
SatRide: 3D Satellite Tracker
Developer String
Play with satellites on a live 3D Earth. Track passes, ISS alerts and AR sky.
Thousands of satellites are flying over your head right now. SatRide is where you go and play with them — spin a live 3D Earth, pick one out of the swarm, and ride along on its shoulder while the planet turns underneath you.
PLAY WITH SATELLITES
A live 3D Earth with hundreds of objects in their real orbits, every one of them where it actually is this second. Drag to spin the planet, pinch to fly in, tap a satellite to lock onto it — then ride along from its point of view and watch the world roll past beneath you. This is the part people show their friends.
POINT AT THE SKY
Hold your phone up and the satellites are there, drawn over your camera view where they actually are. Tap one to follow it, and SatRide guides you to it with a gentle nudge as you get closer. Prefer not to use the camera? Turn it off and the same sky is drawn on a dark background.
KNOW WHEN TO GO OUTSIDE
Every pass over your location, on a timeline: when it rises, how high it climbs, how long it lasts. Set a reminder and SatRide nudges you a few minutes before, so you are outside and looking up when it happens. A bright ISS pass is worth setting an alarm for, and now you do not have to.
EVERY SATELLITE, LISTED
Browse the catalog by what is up now, the popular ones, weather satellites, amateur radio satellites or crewed missions. Every entry has its orbit, its next pass, its downlink frequencies and where it is this second.
WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
A full satellite catalog ships inside the app. It works on a hillside with no bars — no signal needed to know what is overhead. When you do have a connection it quietly refreshes the orbital data so predictions stay sharp.
REAL DATA, REAL ORBITS
Positions are propagated with SGP4 from public orbital element sets published by CelesTrak, with transmitter details from the SatNOGS database. These are the same models professional tracking software uses. SatRide is for observation and enjoyment, not navigation or anything safety-critical.
BUILT TO RESPECT YOU
No account required — everything works signed out. Your location is used on your phone and is never uploaded. The camera draws the sky behind the satellites and records nothing: no photo, no video, ever. No ads.
WHAT YOU NEED
Location, so SatRide knows which satellites are above you — or enter a position by hand instead. Camera, only for the augmented sky view. Notifications, only for pass reminders you ask for. Each one is explained before it is asked for, each is optional, and each can be changed later.
